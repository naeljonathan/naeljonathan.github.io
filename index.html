About

Between the business and the build.

I started as a developer on an internet banking platform, moved into business intelligence and analysis, and now run projects end to end as both BA and PM. Having written code and built dashboards myself makes it easier to sit with operations teams on one side and developers or vendors on the other, and keep both pointed at the same outcome.

Most of my current work replaces spreadsheets and paper logs with platforms that hold up on a mine site: compliance, asset management, hauling and project tracking. A rollout is not done at go-live for me. It is done when every target unit is using it, which on my projects has typically meant within two months.

How I deliver

Five stages, one owner.

The same sequence runs through every project I lead, whether it is software, IoT hardware or a physical command centre.

STAGE 1

Discover

Structured workshops with each department to map how the work really happens today.

STAGE 2

Define

BRD, functional specs and UI wireframes that business and IT both sign off.

STAGE 3

Build

Scope, timeline and quality control with developers, vendors and contractors.

STAGE 4

Test

SIT and UAT coordination so defects are found before users find them.

STAGE 5

Adopt

Training, adoption monitoring and post go-live support until usage is complete.
